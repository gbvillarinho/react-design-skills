# AI tells in copy

Check every headline, subhead and paragraph against this list. A single match is not a crime; a text full of them reads as generated. Fix by replacing the general claim with a concrete fact.

## Structural patterns

- **Slogan antithesis and rhythm.** "Real bread, made with time." "Not just X, it's Y." "Less noise, more flavor." Two balanced halves with a comma or period in the middle.
- **Rule of three.** Lists of exactly three adjectives or benefits ("fresh, local, honest"). Vary the count, or drop the list.
- **The "not X, but Y" reveal** and the rhetorical question that the next sentence answers.
- **Every section opens with a tagline and a one-line promise**, then repeats the same pattern.
- **Uniform paragraph shape.** Each block is three similar sentences of similar length.
- **Stacked fragments for drama.** "Slow. Careful. Yours."

## Words and phrases

- Vague superlatives and quality words: authentic, artisanal, premium, passionate, crafted, curated, seamless, effortless, elevate, unlock, discover, journey, experience, world-class.
- Empty intensifiers: truly, simply, really, just, genuinely.
- Portuguese equivalents: "de verdade", "feito com carinho", "feito com amor", "experiência única", "qualidade que você merece", "sua melhor escolha", "transforme", "descubra", "venha conhecer", "do jeito que você gosta".
- Claims of care or tradition with nothing behind them: "made with care", "tradition and quality", "há anos" without a number.

## Formatting habits

- Em dashes used as a rhythm device, many per page.
- Emoji or sparkle symbols as decoration.
- Title Case headlines in languages that use sentence case.
- Tracked all-caps taglines above headlines.

## What to do instead

| Tell | Replace with |
|---|---|
| "Authentic, artisanal bread" | What makes it so: "24-hour natural fermentation, stone oven, 6 am bake" |
| "Real bread, made with time." | A plain statement of the fact: "Our dough rests for 24 hours." |
| "Discover our story" | The story's first concrete fact, or drop the section |
| "Quality you deserve" | The measurable thing: price, size, ingredients, guarantee |
| Three adjectives | One specific detail, or two or four items that are all true |
| "Há anos cuidando do seu pão" | The year or number: "Desde 2011, no mesmo endereço" |

## Test

Cover the business name. If the line could belong to any competitor, it is generic. Rewrite until it could only belong to this one.
