# Composition and the "one twist"

A page can use fresh colors and type and still look like every other generated page if its structure is the default. Decide the composition on purpose.

## Compare compositions before choosing

In the plan, sketch at least two different compositions in ASCII and pick one that is not the default split hero plus stacked sections. Say why it fits this subject. Some directions to consider, as thinking aids and not a menu:

- **Type as the hero.** One huge headline or word that fills the viewport, with everything else small.
- **Asymmetry and overlap.** Elements that break the grid, overlap, or bleed off the edge.
- **The object is the layout.** The page takes the shape of something from the subject: a price list, a ticket, a schedule board, a spec sheet, a map, a timeline.
- **Scale contrast.** One thing very large next to very small supporting text.
- **A different reading path.** A single long column, a horizontal scroll, a sticky side panel, or a page built around one interaction.
- **Cropped real content.** A tight crop of a real photo or document instead of a full image block.

## One twist per page

Pick one surprising device and carry it through the page. It does not need to be extreme; a small distinct choice already separates a page from the default. Examples: a live element tied to the subject (the next bake time from the visitor's clock), an unusual use of scale, a section that changes the layout rhythm, or a single memorable interaction.

Keep everything around the twist quiet so it reads clearly.

## Imagery and illustration

See `imagery.md` for the full guidance. In short:

- **Do not draw complex objects in CSS or SVG.** Food, people, products and scenes end up crude. If a photo or illustration would help, leave a clearly marked placeholder with its aspect ratio and a short description of the ideal image, and tell the person what to supply.
- Use what you can do well: typography, color fields, patterns, simple geometric forms, real data, diagrams and charts.
- An honest placeholder is better than a bad drawing.
